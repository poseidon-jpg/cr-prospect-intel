## 13. The wedge
Oura has a perception problem with a deadline (the IPO refile) and a CEO with hours of on-message footage nobody is distributing. Clipping is the cheapest way to put that footage in front of tens of millions of people, and pay-per-view makes the test easy to approve.

## 14. Campaign theses
**Demo campaign: "Tom Hale on Sleep".** The full spec is in `cheat-sheet.md`: footage list, hooks, rules, $1/1K, $25K over 4 weeks ≈ 22.5M paid views, win/kill thresholds, the DreamMe page to open, and the close question.

**Thesis 2: "Game-day Readiness" (athletes).**
- **Source:** Oura's own US Open posts. Coco Gauff's got 20.9K views [C015]; Fritz's got 8.8K and 9.4K.
- **What clippers post:** 10-20 second cuts from Oura-owned footage only.
- **Payout:** $1.50/1K.
- **Rules:** no athlete likeness outside Oura's footage. Check the athlete contracts first.
- **Test:** $10K.
- **Win:** a median of 5K+ views per clip.

**Thesis 3: "Ring 5 in real life" (UGC repurposing).**
- **Model:** CR's own Split-Screen UGC campaign at $3/1K (seed library).
- **Source:** customer reviews and unboxings.
- **Rules:** comfort, design and battery only. No health outcomes.
- **Test:** $10K, timed to holiday gifting.
