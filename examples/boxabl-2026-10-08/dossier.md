## 1. Executive snapshot
- **Boxabl is already a Content Rewards customer.** Clip Farm runs "Boxabl Official Clipping" at $0.50/1K across TikTok, IG, YouTube and X: $19,968 spent of $85,000, 836 clippers, ~34M views [C015][C016]. This is an expansion call, not a new logo.
- **Why now:** Boxabl listed on Nasdaq (BXBL) on 2026-07-20 via a $3.5B SPAC [C001]. Shares jumped 20% then gave it back [C003], and headlines reported a 50% fall within days [C004]. It has 50,000+ retail investors [C002] and every one of them watches the ticker.
- **Owned distribution is split.** TikTok @boxabl (114.4K followers) has been silent since 2026-08-24 [C008][C009]. YouTube Shorts post about twice a week [C011]. The clip campaign is carrying short-form while the owned TikTok sleeps.
- **The risk the rep should raise first:** unofficial clip accounts tell viewers to "Invest Now!" in a newly public stock [C013]. That is a disclosure problem for a public company, and CR can fix it with brief rules.
- **Recommended first move:** propose a compliance-safe v2 brief on the existing campaign (no investment language, product and factory only, required disclosure) and pace the $65K remaining before talking about new budget.

## 14. Campaign theses (2-4)
1. **Brief v2, compliance-safe (do first).** Same $0.50/1K. Bans investment language ("invest", "stock", "$BXBL", price talk) and requires product and factory framing plus a disclosure tag. It protects the program and makes it easier to scale.
2. **Third-party proof clips.** Clip the contractor, owner-review and delivery videos (YouTube's top formats [C011]). Pay a bonus CPM on clips that name the Casita price with its caveats [C005].
3. **Founder long-form → clips.** Put Paolo on 4-6 podcasts this quarter. Clip them at $1/1K with the founder as the face. This repeats the Higgsfield 20VC and Trailblazers pattern (seed library).
4. **Server Pod B2B test.** A small application-only campaign aimed at tech and finance clippers, using the data-center concept [C022]. It only runs if Boxabl is pushing that line.
